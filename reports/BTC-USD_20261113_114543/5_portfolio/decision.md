The Neutral Analyst's own technical read — slowing volume into a 3-week grind rather than a 1-2 day flush — favors the "grinding bear with false bounces" pattern over the "sharp capitulation reverses immediately" pattern, which undercuts the aggressive timing claim.

On flows and positioning, $14.2B in 30-day stablecoin outflows (the largest since 2022) alongside concurrent ETF outflows is a heavier, more current signal than the bull-side's accumulation narrative. JPMorgan simultaneously increasing MicroStrategy puts by 60% while adding 36% more shares is more consistent with defensive hedging of a position whose floor is uncertain than with confident dip-buying — if the floor were believed to be in, the hedge ratio would not be scaled up this aggressively. Bitdeer's $125.5M net loss while still holding mined BTC looks more like forced/distressed holding than voluntary conviction accumulation. On macro, 98% odds of zero 2026 Fed cuts plus only 60% probability (not 85%+) of $100k by Dec 2027 is a tail risk split that does not support the aggressive claim that the hawkish backdrop is "fully priced in" — if it were, confidence in the recovery target would be higher, not a near-coinflip over two years.

The bull/aggressive case has real elements — the halving cycle, extreme technical readings, and smart-money accumulation signals are genuine — but the halving is 16 months out and is a 2027/2028 catalyst, mechanically dwarfed by the current daily stablecoin outflow run-rate. This is why the call is Underweight rather than Sell: full capitulation exit would ignore the legitimate tail risk of missing a bottom entirely, and the Neutral Analyst's probability framing (55-60% near-term bounce odds vs. 40-45% further decline) is genuinely close enough that zeroing out exposure isn't justified. But the weight of current, verifiable evidence — confirmed downtrend structure across all moving averages, accelerating outflows, hawkish Fed with low conviction on near-term recovery, and defensive institutional hedging — favors trimming now and re-engaging only on confirmed capitulation-exhaustion signals rather than adding into an unconfirmed bounce.

What would change this call: sustained reversal of stablecoin/ETF flows to net positive for multiple sessions, MACD histogram compression and bullish crossover, RSI reclaiming and holding above 40, and price reclaiming the 200 SMA (~$73,068) would justify scaling back toward full allocation. Conversely, a break below $62,000 with continued outflow acceleration would argue for moving from Underweight toward Sell.
