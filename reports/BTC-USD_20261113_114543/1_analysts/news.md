# **Comprehensive Trading Report: BTC-USD (Bitcoin USD)**
## Week of November 6–13, 2026

---

## **Executive Summary**

**BTC-USD** is under significant pressure as of November 13, 2026, with the market facing a confluence of headwinds: deteriorating technical support, large-scale liquidations, stablecoin outflows, and a hawkish Fed stance. The asset has fallen to 3-month lows amid risk-off sentiment, macro uncertainty, and capital rotation toward AI-related sectors. Prediction markets show virtually no priced-in Fed rate cuts for 2026, and Bitcoin faces formidable obstacles to re-establishing $100k support within the calendar year.

---

## **1. Market Action & Technical Deterioration**

### Key Developments:

- **Liquidation Cascade:** Nearly $600 million in crypto longs liquidated as BTC-USD fell to August lows (3-month lows), signaling aggressive de-risking by leveraged traders.
- **Technical Breakdown:** Bitcoin lost its critical **200-day moving average**, a key support level. Analysts are targeting **$67,000** as the next meaningful support level.
- **Stablecoin Liquidity Crisis:** Stablecoin supply contracted by **$14.2 billion in 30 days**—the largest warning signal since 2022—compounded by growing ETF outflows. This signals diminishing purchasing power to drive price rallies.

### Implication:  
The combination of technical breakdown, forced liquidations, and collapsing stablecoin reserves suggests capitulation may be underway, but bottom formation could take weeks to establish.

---

## **2. Macroeconomic Context: Fed Policy & Rate Expectations**

### Fed Rate Outlook (Prediction Markets):

Polymarket data reveals an **unambiguous hawkish skew**:

- **98% probability** that **NO Fed rate cuts occur in 2026** ($9.6M volume, highest liquidity)
- **0.05–0.55%** probability of any meaningful rate cuts (1, 6, 9, 10+ cuts all <1% priced)

### Risk Environment:

- **High Bond Yields:** Gold has fallen to **13-month lows** amid **$96 oil** fueling expectations for "bigger Fed hikes" (not cuts)—a bearish signal for inflation expectations and risk appetite.
- **No Recession Priced In:** US recession by end-2026 carries only **2% probability** in prediction markets, with the 1-week move down -2.2pp, indicating markets expect continued policy tightening without economic collapse.

### Implication:  
A tightening Fed with no rate cuts priced in is structurally hostile to risk assets, especially non-yielding assets like Bitcoin. This explains the recent de-risking and explains why BTC-USD has underperformed risk-off sentiment.

## **3. Capital Flows & Institutional Positioning**

### Negative Signals:

- **MicroStrategy (MSTR) Regulatory Shock:** MSCI Global Standard Index removing MicroStrategy—arguably the largest Bitcoin treasury holder ($19.5B profit generated)—due to its substantial Bitcoin holdings not qualifying as traditional "business" operations. This threatens:
  - Benchmark-tracking flows into MSTR (and indirectly BTC-USD)
  - Confidence in Bitcoin's "institutional acceptance"
  
- **JPMorgan Hedge Positioning:** While JPMorgan increased MicroStrategy shares 36% ahead of MSCI exclusion, they simultaneously **increased put options 60%**—a clear bet against a further decline.

### Positive Signals (Weak):

- **Institutional Support:** Barclays and Citigroup increased stakes in BlackRock's Bitcoin ETF (IBIT), and Europe's largest asset manager doubled down on MicroStrategy. However, this is **outweighed by aggregate outflows**.

### Implication:  
Institutional interest remains fragmented. While some mega-cap banks support, regulatory headwinds (MSCI removal) and macro uncertainty prevent sustained buying. ETF outflows are a primary concern.

---

## **4. Sectoral & Thematic Headwinds**

### AI Capital Rotation:

- **Nvidia CEO Resignation Crisis:** Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO, triggering an immediate **14% stock decline** and dragging Bitcoin miners exposed to AI infrastructure (Cipher, IREN, Terawulf) down **5–10%**.
- **Capital Flight from Crypto to AI:** Kevin O'Leary (Shark Tank) explicitly cited institutional capital holding back from crypto **pending clearer Washington regulations**, with AI drawing incremental investment instead.

### Mining Sector Mixed Signals:

- **Bitdeer Q3:** Record $320M quarterly revenue but **net loss widened to $125.5M**, highlighting cost pressures and low profitability in current price environment.
- **Hive Q1 2027 Target:** $500M annual run-rate goal (mining + AI cloud), but execution risk remains high in a low-BTC-price environment.

### Implication:  
Mining economics are deteriorating. With BTC-USD weakness and AI drawing institutional capital, mining companies face margin compression—a risk factor that could suppress network security investment if hashrates decline.

## **5. Bitcoin-Specific Sentiment & Medium-Term Outlooks**

### Analyst Outlooks (Mixed):

- **Joe Burnett (Strive VP of Bitcoin Strategy):** Argues Fed rate hikes don't matter for Bitcoin long-term; sees easing liquidity before 2028 halving setting up strong 2027. However, he's in a minority.
- **Analyst Warnings:** Multiple analysts liken Bitcoin ETF slump to gold, citing broken technicals and consensus re-risk-off to traditional safe havens rather than cryptoassets.

### Long-Term Narrative (2028–2036):

- Some strategists (Strive) see Bitcoin reaching **$11M per coin by 2036** based on supply dynamics and 2028 halving, but this requires reversing current momentum and regaining institutional confidence.

### Implication:  
Medium-term sentiment is pessimistic; long-term upside narratives exist but are discounted by near-term macro headwinds.

## **7. Regulatory & Geopolitical Uncertainty**

### Implication:  
Regulatory clarity is a gate-keeping factor for large institutional flows. Without it, BTC-USD remains a risk asset vulnerable to macro hawkishness.

## **Conclusion for Traders**

**BTC-USD is in a near-term downtrend with multiple structural headwinds:**

1. **Macro:** Fed rate hike certainty (98% no cuts) removes technical floor for risk assets.
2. **Technicals:** Loss of 200-day MA signals continued weakness toward $67k.
3. **Flows:** Stablecoin outflows and ETF outflows indicate institutional de-risking.
4. **Sentiment:** Capital rotation to AI (Nvidia, CoreWeave) away from crypto.
5. **Regulation:** Undefined Washington stance and MSCI removal create uncertainty.

**Trading recommendation:** 
- **Short-term (6–8 weeks):** High probability BTC-USD tests $67k; consider ranges or defensive shorts.
- **Medium-term (6–12 months):** 60% market probability of $100k by Dec 2027 suggests capitulation zone ($60–70k) may offer asymmetric long opportunity IF macro improves or regulatory clarity emerges.
- **Catalysts to watch:** Fed pivot announcement, Washington crypto rules, stablecoin reflow, 2028 halving momentum.
